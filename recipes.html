Coffee e-book recipes

A practical collection of recipes + templates designed for beginners (and ADHD brains). Copy the structure, then tweak one variable at a time.

1) Espresso “Dial-In” Template

Start here when changing beans or humidity.

  • Goal: balanced shot (sweet, not sour / not bitter)
  • Record: Dose / Yield / Time / Grind
  • Rule: change only one thing per shot
BeginnerEspressoConsistency

2) Flat White (Beginner Friendly)

Simple, forgiving, and great for latte art practice.

  • Recipe: double espresso + silky microfoam
  • Texture cue: “wet paint” shine, no big bubbles
  • Tip: keep it small to control the pour
MilkLatte artDaily driver

3) Iced Latte (Fast Workflow)

Hot weather friendly — minimal steps.

  • Build: ice + cold milk
  • Then: pour espresso over (or shake if you want)
  • Tip: slightly shorter yield helps flavor stay strong
IcedSummerQuick

4) Latte Art Drills (10 min)

Small drills that build muscle memory.

  • Drill A: texture only (no pour), learn the sound + timing
  • Drill B: center pour → heart (repeat 5x)
  • Drill C: slow wiggle → rosetta (repeat 5x)
